The Softball Ready standard

Community Standards

Opportunity grows when people communicate honestly, respectfully, and with the well-being of athletes at the center.

Be Honest

Keep player, team, tryout, and roster information accurate and current.

Be Respectful

Communicate with families, players, and coaches the way you would want someone to communicate with your own family.

Protect Athletes

Use parent-managed contact and private messaging. Never misuse personal information.

Remove Filled Needs

Close listings promptly so families do not waste time pursuing unavailable opportunities.

No Harassment

Threatening, discriminatory, deceptive, or abusive conduct has no place in this community.

Leave the Game Better

Every interaction should strengthen the softball community, not take advantage of it.

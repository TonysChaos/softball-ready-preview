A father's story

Our Story

Softball Ready was not created from a business plan. It grew from years of family experiences at practices, ballparks, tournaments, and long drives before sunrise.

It all started on Field 1 at Sandy Plains Softball Park in 2014. Our daughter was seven years old, one of four children at the time in our newly blended family. We signed her up for 8U fastpitch as a “let's see how she likes this” kind of thing.

Our oldest son played baseball, and two of our daughters were in cheer. We were completely new to fastpitch softball. We honestly did not even know it existed until then. So I bought her a T-ball bat and a pink glove.

I will never forget her first practice. Midway through it, her coach walked over holding that brand-new bat and said, “I think she broke it.” Sure enough, she did.

Softball did not come naturally to her right away. It took several years before everything finally clicked. Around ten or eleven, my wife and I even considered taking her out because she did not seem to be progressing as quickly as the girls around her. Then, near the beginning of 12U, something changed. From that point forward, she became one of the best players on the field.

Around the same time, we welcomed another daughter into our family. She began playing at about three and a half years old and grew up looking toward her older sister. Over the years, we raised five children, coached many teams, practiced in the backyard, and made memories that became some of the most meaningful parts of our lives.

There were long tournament drives that began before sunrise while our oldest daughter slept in the truck. There were friendships that became lifelong friendships. Eventually, we watched her earn the opportunity to play college softball. Today, her younger sister is chasing that same dream.

Throughout all those years, one problem kept returning: finding the right team. It often depended on knowing someone who knew someone whose daughter heard about an opening. Social media helped, but it was still scattered, incomplete, and mostly organized word-of-mouth.

Softball Ready was created from that experience. It is not just a website. It is a life experience transformed into a simple, welcoming community designed to help players, families, coaches, and teams who share the same passion for softball.

We know these years do not last forever. One day, the last tournament will end, the last ride to practice will happen, and the final game will be played. If Softball Ready can make those years a little easier, less stressful, and more joyful for the families who come after us, then we will have done something worthwhile.

Welcome to Softball Ready.
Welcome home.

God Bless,

Tony Puleo
Founder, Softball Ready
